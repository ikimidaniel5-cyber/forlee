## Thumbnail system (the same for every video)
- Plain background.
- Title at the top, highlighted, in Vox-style cut-out lettering.
- Celebrity in the centre with a black bar over the eyes.
- Estimated 2025 earnings, **larger than the title**, in Vox lettering.
- Income-stream logos around the celebrity: the main stream fully visible, the others partly hidden. The number of logos varies per celebrity.

## Editing Rules (from Nathan's PDF)
**Pacing**
- Micro-motion every **2–5 s**: bills moving, paper people walking, camera push or pull, arrows drawing, labels stamping, products sliding, numbers counting, photos separating into layers.
- A meaningful **composition change every 8–15 s** (YouTube screen → ledger → retail shelf → corporate ecosystem → ownership map → Money Map).
- An **intellectual change every 45–75 s**: a new contradiction, business, number, question or financial concept.
- Heavy **scale changes**: one chocolate bar to millions of customers, one viewer to hundreds of millions of eyes, one dollar to company revenue.
- Not "paper texture on everything". **The motion has to explain the economics.**

**Visual vocabulary (fixed for all 12 episodes)**
| Concept | Visual |
|---|---|
| Contradiction ("but", "except", "the problem", "here's where it gets interesting") | Paper tear / rip transition |
| Classification or fact | Stamp: REVENUE · PROFIT · VALUATION · EQUITY · ESTIMATE · REPORTED · OWNED |
| Money | Physical bills, coins, paper numbers |
| Revenue / Expense / Profit | Money flowing in / money flowing out / money left behind |
| Ownership / Equity | Certificate or pie slice / percentage attached to a company |
| Valuation | **Price tag on the whole company, never a pile of cash next to the celebrity** |
| Attention / Audience | Eyeballs, cameras, phones pointed at the celebrity / paper crowd |
| Brand / Company | Product packaging around the celebrity / paper building or storefront |
| Salary / Royalties / Licensing | Paycheck / small recurring arrows / celebrity stamp pressed onto someone else's product |
| IP | Document, stamp, © symbol |
| Reported or estimated (not confirmed) | **Dotted border** |
| Evidence | Article clipping with the figure highlighted, then the number cut out and flown into the graphic |
| "It's bigger than you assumed" | Camera pull-back |

**Source snippets:** whenever a major number is cited, bring the article or document into frame, highlight the figure, cut it out, and animate it into the graphic.
